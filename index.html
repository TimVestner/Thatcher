The Thatcher Illusion

Fit your head and shoulders inside the outline, look at the camera and press Take photo.
Tip: Taking off glasses, if you're wearing them, makes the effect stronger.

Are the green boxes around your eyes and mouth?

If a box is slightly off, you can drag it into place.

What happened? In the right-hand photo, your eyes and mouth were flipped upside down. When a face is upside down, your brain mainly checks the separate parts (eyes, nose, mouth), and they look fine. When the face is the right way up, your brain sees the face as a whole and notices right away that something is badly wrong. This is the Thatcher illusion, discovered by Peter Thompson in 1980 using a photo of Margaret Thatcher.
